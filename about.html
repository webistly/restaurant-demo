Our Story

Where It All Began

Ember & Spice was born from a simple belief: that great food should be an experience, not just a meal. Founded in 2016 by Chef Adrian Cole, our restaurant is a tribute to the marriage of fire and flavor, tradition and innovation.

What started as a small pop-up kitchen has grown into a warm, inviting space where every guest is treated like family. We source local, cook with intention, and serve with pride.

Meet Our Chef

Chef Adrian Cole

With over 15 years of culinary experience across Michelin-starred kitchens, Chef Adrian brings a passion for bold flavors and refined technique to Ember & Spice. His philosophy is simple: let the ingredients speak, and let the fire do the work.

Adrian believes that dining should be an adventure — one that tells a story with every course. He personally crafts each menu, drawing inspiration from global cuisines and local farms.
